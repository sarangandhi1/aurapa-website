What to include

Three things let us give you a useful answer quickly: what you are trying to do, roughly what deposit or equity you have, and how you earn your income. If a lender has already declined you, say which one and why if you know it.

What happens next

One of our team calls you for ten minutes to understand the case. If it is workable we say so and explain the route. If it is not workable yet we say that too, and tell you what would change the answer.
