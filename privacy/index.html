The legal bit

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Aurapa Ltd collects, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected.

Who we are

Aurapa Ltd is a commercial finance brokerage based in Coventry, England. We are an Appointed Representative of Commercial Finance Network Corporation Ltd, which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority under number 796413.

What we collect

When you enquire through our website, our calculators, our advertising forms or by phone, we may collect your name, contact details, and financial and business information necessary to assess and process a finance enquiry, such as income, deposit, property details and credit history you choose to share.

Why we collect it

We use this information to assess your enquiry, to contact you about it, to introduce you to advisers within the Commercial Finance Network and to lenders where appropriate, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations. The legal bases are your consent, steps taken at your request before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in operating a brokerage.

Who we share it with

We share your information with Commercial Finance Network Corporation Ltd and with lenders relevant to your case. We do not sell your information. Service providers that process data for us, such as form and messaging providers, do so under their own published safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to handle your case and to meet regulatory record keeping requirements, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact s.gandhi@cfnuk.com or write to us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
